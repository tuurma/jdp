    Chapter 4 Formal structure of the text

    Every text is represented as a series of div elements, which may be further nested, following the logical structure of the text. Each of the divisions comprises of an obligatory head element, followed by a number of p (paragraphs). Structural, top level elements (direct children) of the division may also include marginal notes, arguments or epigraphs.

    Selected elements of the physical structure of the document, namely page (and potentially column) beginnings may also occur.

    The example below shows a simplified example, illustrating the top-level components of a division.

    <div type="life" xml:id="zs0217.life">
  <pb n="631"/>
  <note type="other" place="margin">18 Julii – lipca </note>
  <head type="title">
Żywot świętego Fryderyka męczennika, biskupa trajekteńskiego...
</head>
  <p n="1">Fryderyk rodem z Fryzyjej...</p>
  <p n="2">Po zeszciu Rykfryda księża i lud wszytek...</p>
</div>

    4.1 Identification and classification

    The div element may carry the following attributes:

    • @xml:id: an identifier (obligatory)
    • @type: division type, e.g. sermon or editorial
    • @n: ordinal (a number)

    4.2 Division title (head)

    Each of the sections represented by a div MUST have a head providing its title. Where such a heading doesn't appear in the source text, it MUST be supplied and explicitly marked up with supplied element.

    <head>
  <supplied>Psalm 1</supplied>
</head>

    Division heading plays an important additional function of providing a label for the table of contents or search results. Where original heading would be impractical for such a purpose, alternative heading supplied by the editor should be provided.

    <head type="title">
  <choice>
    <orig>Żywot świętego Fryderyka męczennika, biskupa trajekteńskiego, wzięty z kościoła tegoż
a z dawna pisany, położony u Suryjusa.<seg type="floruit">Żył około roku
Pańskiego 820.</seg></orig>
    <reg type="toc">Żywot świętego Fryderyka</reg>
  </choice>
</head>

    4.3 Epigraphs and arguments

    In some of the collections, particularly sermons, epigraphs or arguments may occur. These may be encoded as epigraph or argument elements, respectively.

    <epigraph>
  <cit type="bible" corresp="BRXAKCWM">
    <quote>Tyś jest, co masz przyść, czy-li inszego czekamy?</quote>
  </cit>
</epigraph>

    <argument>
  <ab>O naprawie stanu małżeńskiego przez Chrystusa</ab>
</argument>
