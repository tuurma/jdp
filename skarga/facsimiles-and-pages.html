    Chapter 6 Facsimiles and pages

    Beginnings of each page in the primary source are to be encoded with pb element.

    Columns and lines of the primary source are not represented.

    Page beginning markers must be placed as a first node in a div, or wherever they occurr in the source text.

    Page beginnings must have an @n attribute.

    <div type="life" xml:id="zs0217.life">
  <pb n="631"/>
  <head type="title">Żywot...</head>
</div>

    All page elements are assumed to link to corresponding files at the UJ IIIF Hub.
