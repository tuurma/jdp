    Chapter 8 General organization of the metadata (teiHeader)

    Metadata section records information specific to the text as a whole. Since all texts in a subcollection originate from the same work, they share a lot of this information. Therefore their individual metadata headers only explicitly record what is specific to the text in question, and only point to the canonical description of the shared parts.

    The shared information includes:

    • publicationStmtgiving the information about the digital publication itself
    • the sourceDesc, listing all extant witnesses
    • licensing information

    The header section is divided principally into the fileDesc and profileDesc, described below.
