    Pisma Piotra Skargi: TEI Customization

    This TEI ODD defines a TEI customization closely tailored to the needs of the edition of collected works of Piotr Skarga: lives of saints, sermons and polemical writings.

    The tightly constrained schema aims to represent the critical overview of the extant versions of the work, enriched with extensive philological commentary and identification of intertextual references.

    Designed for: Jagiellonian Digital Platform (Jagiellońska Platforma Cyfrowa)
