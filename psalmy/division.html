    Chapter 3 Anatomy of a psalm

    Every psalm is represented as a div element with certain obligatory attributes. Internally, it comprises of an obligatory head element, followed by a number of ab, representing canonical verses of the Book of Psalms.

    The example below shows the initial verse of Psalm 1

    <div type="psalm" n="1" xml:id="PF-001" corresp="commentary-ps-001" synch="ps-001" ana="#wisdom">
  <pb facs="334664cd-855a-4ffd-b87b-757d5c6841f6" n="3r" xml:id="PF-pb-001"/>
  <head>
    <supplied>Psalm 1</supplied>
  </head>
  <ab type="verse" n="1" xml:id="PF-001-01" synch="ps-001-01"><lb/> Beatus vir qui non abiit
<lb/> inconsilio impiorum et
<lb/> inuia peccatorum non
<lb/> stetit et inkathedra
<lb/> pestilencie non sedit
</ab>
</div>

    3.1 The psalm (div)

    The div element representing a psalm must have a number of obligatory attributes. These are:

    • @xml:id: a psalm identifier
    • @synch: reference to the canonical verse identifier
    • @ana: reference(s) to psalm classification taxonomy
    • @type: psalm
    • @n: verse ordinal (a number)

    3.2 Psalm title (head)

    Each of the psalms div MUST have a head providing the psalm's title. Where it doesn't appear in the source text, it must be supplied and additionally marked up with supplied element.

    3.3 Verse (ab)

    Each psalm must have at least one verse, encoded with ab element. Each verse must have a number of obligatory attributes. These are:

    • @xml:id: a verse identifier
    • @corresp: pointer to a psalm introductory article
    • @synch: reference to the canonical psalm identifier
    • @ana: reference(s) to psalm classification taxonomy
    • @type: verse
    • @n: psalm ordinal (a number)

    3.4 Epigraphs and arguments

    In some of the editions, non-canonical content may occur. These may be encoded as epigraphs or arguments (with ab element, but marked as type="argument").

    <epigraph>
  <foreign xml:lang="la">Quare fremuerunt</foreign>
</epigraph>

    <ab type="argument">Ten psalm powiada, iże Krystus w dziedzictwo bierze
wszystek lud od Boga Ojca Dawidowo.</ab>
