    att.global.responsibility

    provides attributes indicating the agent responsible for some aspect of the text, the markup or something asserted by the markup, and the degree of certainty associated with it.

    Module tei

    Attributes

    @certsignifies the degree of certainty associated with the intervention or interpretation.
    Status
    Optional
    Datatype
    teidata.probCert
    @respindicates the agency responsible for the intervention or interpretation, for example an editor or transcriber.
    Status
    Optional
    Datatype
    1–∞ occurrences of teidata.pointer separated by whitespace
