    Chapter 1 Identifiers and referencing mechanisms

    Identifiers, represented as @xml:id attributes on appropriate elements follow a consistent naming scheme across the Psalter editions, witnesses, psalms and annotation layers.

    • Edition (Psalter) siglum: e.g. PK (Psałterz krakowski), or PF (Psałterz floriański)
    • Witness siglum (used in the critical apparatus entries): constructed from the edition siglum followed by year, e.g. PK1532, PK1535
    • Psalm number: sequential number, padded to 3 digits, e.g. 001 for Psalm 1
    • Canonical psalm identifier (shared across editions): ps- followed by the psalm number, e.g. ps-001 (for Psalm 1). Used as the value of @synch on div.
    • Psalm introductory article identifier (shared across editions): commentary-ps-001 (for the introduction to Psalm 1). Used as the value of @corresp on div.
    • Psalm identifier (in a given edition): edition siglum followed by the psalm number, e.g. PK-001 (Psalm 1 in Psałterz krakowski)
    • Canonical verse identifier (shared across editions): ps-001-01 (Psalm 1, verse 1).
    • Verse identifier (in a given edition): PK-001-01 (Psalm 1, verse 1 in Psałterz krakowski).
    • Incipit (in a given edition): PK-001-incipit.

    Identifiers are defined exactly once across the dataset, where they occur as a value of @xml:id attribute on an XML element

    Identifiers may be used multiple times when they are referenced from another element. This may be done via a number of pointing attributes, such as @corresp, @synch, etc. Each of these attributes has a specific function across the Psalters dataset.

    1.1 Registers

    References for analytical features can be resolved against appropriate registers.

    • versions.xml: catalogue of editions (psalters) with their identifiers, names and ordinal numbers.
    • lexicon.xml: dictionary of lexical explanations; identifiers are defined on sense and entry elements (e.g. lex-stolec-1) and pointed to via @corresp.
    • commentary.xml: psalm introductory articles (e.g. commentary-ps-001) and pointed to via div/@corresp.
    • phonetics.xml: register of phonetic phenomena with explanations (e.g. phon-wzdluzenie), pointed to via m/@ana.
    • morphology.xml: morphological classification; register of identifiers from the NKJP tagset; referenced by individual labels in w/@msd.
    • taxonomy.xml: metadata classifications, e.g. psalm types (div/@ana) and kinds of translation (catRef/@target).
