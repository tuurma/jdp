    Chapter 6 Morphological analysis

    Only the regularized forms (reg) are subject to morphological analysis. A w element is always used as a wrapper to provide the analysis with @lemma for the base form and @msd for NKJP-style morphological annotation.

    Classifications in @msd are resolved against the Morphology register.

    <reg>
  <w lemma="Niemiłościwy" msd="adj:pl:gen:m:pos:compos">niemiłościwych</w>
</reg>
