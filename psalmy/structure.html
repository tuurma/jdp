    Chapter 2 General structure of the Psalter

    Each of the Psalters, e.g. Psałterz floriański, or Psałterz krakowski is represented as an individual TEI document.

    Such a document is composed of three parts: the teiHeader, standOff and text sections.

    The TEI element must carry an obligatory @xml:id attribute with a psalter identifier, e.g. PK.

    The div element is used as a main organizational unit in the text, representing a single psalm. Thus in the text/body section, nothing but a series of divs may occur.

    The text and body hold the annotated manuscript or printed primary source.

    <TEI xml:lang="pl" xml:id="PK">
  <teiHeader>
...
</teiHeader>
  <standOff>
...
</standOff>
  <text>
    <body><div xml:id="structure-psalm-1"><head>Psalm 1</head>
...
</div><div xml:id="structure-psalm-2"><head>Psalm 2</head>
...
</div>

...
</body>
  </text>
</TEI>
