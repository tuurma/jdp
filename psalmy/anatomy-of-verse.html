    Chapter 4 Anatomy of verse

    A number of high-level phenomena can be encoded as descendants of verse. The elements allowed in verse (ab) are:

    • page (pb), line (lb), and column (cb) beginnings
    • seg: an arbitrary segment
    • w: a word with morphological analysis results
    • choice: phrase with spelling regularization
    • app: phrase with alternate readings across extant witnesses

    4.1 Multi-lingual verses (PF)

    In Psałterz floriański, each verse is repeated three times, in Latin, Polish and German. In this case, each language version is wrapped in a seg element with @xml:lang and @rend attributes. No other content is present between these segments.

    @xml:lang values are la for Latin, de for German and pl for Polish. See the simplified example below.

    <seg xml:lang="la" rend="ab">Beatus vir qui non abiit...</seg>
<seg xml:lang="de" rend="ab">Der zelige man der nicht gink...</seg>

    4.2 Spelling regularization (choice)

    Words or phrases where spelling of the primary source differs from the transcription are wrapped in a choice with orig (for the transliteration) and reg (for transcription).

    Regularized version may include further morphological and phonetic analysis, see further below.

    <choice>
  <orig>møsz</orig>
  <reg>
    <w lemma="Mąż" msd="subst:sg:nom:m">mąż</w>
  </reg>
</choice>

    Where regularized spelling involves multiple orthographic words, each of these MUST be encoded with w, and whitespace between them explicitly encoded with pc. Cf. the example below:

    <choice>
  <orig>neszedl</orig>
  <reg>
    <w lemma="Nie" msd="1:part">nie</w>
    <pc msd="interp"/>
    <w lemma="iść" msd="ppraetact2:sg:nom:m:imperf:aff:ncompos">szedł</w>
  </reg>
</choice>

    4.2.1 Punctuation

    Punctuation should only be marked up where it requires regularization, otherwise it should just be transcribed.

    Care must be taken not to introduce unnecessary whitespace between punctuation and preceding text.

    <choice>
  <orig>
    <pc>/</pc>
  </orig>
  <reg>
    <pc>,</pc>
  </reg>
</choice>
