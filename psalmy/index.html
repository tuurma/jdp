    TEI Psalmy

    This TEI ODD defines a TEI customization closely tailored to the needs of the didactic edition of Polish translations of the Book of Psalms. The tightly constrained schema affords provisions for the representation of alignment across extant text versions, canonical structure of the work across psalms and verses, enhanced with several layers of analysis (morphological and phonetic analysis, spelling regularization, editorial and linguistic commentary).
