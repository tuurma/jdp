    Chapter 7 Phonetic analysis

    Only the regularized forms (reg) are subject to phonetic analysis. Within the w element, smaller segments are marked with m, which is always used as a wrapper to provide the analysis of the phonetic phenomena with @ana.

    References in @ana are resolved against the Phonetic register.

    <reg>
  <w lemma="Droga" msd="subst:sg:loc:f">d<m ana="#phon-metateza">ro</m><m ana="#phon-2-palat">dz</m>e</w>
</reg>

    Multiple phenomena may be linked to the same m, by separating references with space.

    <m ana="#phon-metateza #phon-r-sonant">rze</m>
wo
