    Chapter 8 Editorial commentary (standOff)

    standOff with listAnnotation and note elements to store editorial commentary, linked from the text via @corresp.

    <standOff>
  <listAnnotation>
...
<note xml:id="PF-001-note-002" n="2">po radzie – ‘za radą’</note>
...
</listAnnotation>
</standOff>

    There are two ways to connect the stand-off commentary notes with the source text. When the note concerns a single orthographic word in the source, it should be attached to the choice with a @corresp.

    <choice corresp="PF-001-note-002">
  <orig>poradze</orig>
  <reg>
    <w lemma="Po" msd="prep:loc">po</w>
    <pc msd="interp"/>
    <w lemma="Rada" msd="subst:sg:loc:f">radzie</w>
  </reg>
</choice>

    Where commentary regards a longer phrase, or there's no adequate choice wrapper available, a seg element must be added around the relevant phrase, as required. The link is still established via @corresp attribute.

    <seg corresp="PF-001-note-001">
  <choice>
    <orig>iest</orig>
    <reg>
      <w lemma="Być" msd="1:sg:ter:imperf">jest</w>
    </reg>
  </choice>
  <choice>
    <orig>neszedl</orig>
    <reg>
      <w lemma="Nie" msd="1:part">nie</w>
      <pc msd="interp"/>
      <w lemma="iść" msd="ppraetact2:sg:nom:m:imperf:aff:ncompos">szedł</w>
    </reg>
  </choice>
</seg>
