    Chapter 5 Facsimiles and pages

    Beginnings of each page in the primary source are to be encoded with pb element.

    Columns of the primary source are not represented in this customization.

    Page beginning markers must be placed as a first node in a div, before its head, even if the poem in question starts in the middle of the page. Since individual poem is the overarching unit of organization, it is necessary to assume this convention to represent all the pages of the primary source relevant to the poem.

    All other page beginnings should be placed where they occurr in the source text.

    Page beginnings in the transcription must have a @facs attribute. All page beginnings must furthermore have @synch and @n attributes.

    <pb facs="#f_8r" xml:id="pb.I.1.1" synch="#I.1" n="8r"/>

    Facsimile links are resolved against the facsimile register, by means of the @xml:id, which will fall on the surface element for pages (pb), or zone for verse lines (l).

    The graphic element on the surface provides the filename, corresponding to the assets available through the UJ IIIF Hub.

    <surfaceGrp xml:id="f_8" n="8">
  <surface xml:id="f_8r" n="8r"><graphic url="0012_0008_b.tiff"/><zone xml:id="f_8r.zone-1" ulx="727" uly="735" lrx="1759" lry="800"/><zone xml:id="f_8r.zone-2" ulx="895" uly="1035" lrx="1509" lry="1118"/><zone xml:id="f_8r.zone-3" ulx="546" uly="1178" lrx="1790" lry="1264"/>
...
</surface>
</surfaceGrp>
