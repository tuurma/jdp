    Chapter 4 Formal structure of the morale

    Every morale is represented as a single div element. It comprises of an obligatory head element, followed by one or more of lg (strophes). Structural, top level elements (direct children) of the division may also include marginal notes.

    Selected elements of the physical structure of the document, namely page beginnings may also occur.

    The example below shows a simplified example, illustrating the top-level components of a division.

    <div xml:id="I.1" n="1" synch="#I.1">
  <pb facs="#f_8r" polona="096aba0b-89de-492c-98c7-6e3c047620b4" xml:id="pb.I.1.1"/>
  <head xml:id="I.1.title" synch="#I.1.title" facs="#f_8r.zone-2">
Citra vinum temulentia
<anchor corresp="#I.1.c.1"/></head>
  <note place="margin">Pijaństwo bez wina</note>
  <lg><l xml:id="I.1.1" n="1" synch="#I.1.1" facs="#f_8r.zone-3">Niechaj się nikt nie myli, żeby tylko winem</l><l xml:id="I.1.2" n="2" synch="#I.1.2" facs="#f_8r.zone-4">Abo więc trunkiem ludzie upijali inem;</l>
...
</lg>
</div>

    4.1 Identification and classification

    The div element MUST carry the following attributes:

    • @xml:id: an identifier (obligatory)
    • @synch: synchronization key, same as the identifier of morale transcription
    • @n: ordinal (a number)

    4.2 Morale title (head)

    Each morale MUST have a head providing its title. Where such a heading doesn't appear in the source text, it MUST be supplied and explicitly marked up with supplied element.

    <head>Citra vinum temulentia</head>
