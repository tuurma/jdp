    Chapter 7 Editorial commentary (linked)

    Editorial commentary is always linked to the place it refers to, using anchor elements as wrappers, and @corresp as the reference key.

    Anchors should be always placed within a verse line l or marginalia note they refer to, they MUST NOT be hanging outside of these structural elements.

    7.1 Bibliographical references in the commentary

    Editorial commentary in the Moralia edition explores transmission of ideas, in particular between Erasmus of Rotterdam's Adagia and Potocki's Moralia, but often tracking earlier influences, back to Antiquity. What follows, editorial commentary frequently mentions authors and work titles, and, where possible, includes bibliographical references or links to online resources.

    While the question is still open as to what extent these relationships will be encoded, the first draft proposes to proceed as follows:

    Where specific reference is made, the bibl element is to be used, either linked directly to an external resource or internally, to a bibliographical entry in registers/bibliography.xml. If the former, the @corresp attribute MUST use an absolute URI link, in the latter case a cross-reference using the local identifier, e.g. <bibl corresp="#Hor-Carm"> should be made. In both cases, micro-structure of the reference string should be marked up with title and author elements.

    The example below illustrates the two approaches for the same Plutarch reference:

    <bibl corresp="https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=urn:cts:greekLit:tlg0007.tlg112.perseus-grc1:716a"><author>Plut.</author><title>Quaes. Conv.</title> 716a</bibl>
<bibl corresp="#Plut-QuaesConv"><author>Plut.</author><title>Quaes. Conv.</title> 716a</bibl>

    Where a more general remark about potential relation to other author's work is made, without pinpointing the exact passage, an author's name can be marked up with persName with @role set to author.

    Prawdopodobnie nawiązanie do cytowanego przez
<persName type="author" key="gnd-118530666">Erazma</persName>

<persName type="author" key="gnd-118553569">Horacego</persName>

<foreign xml:lang="lat">fortunaque dulci ebria</foreign>

    7.2 Quotes, paraphrases and translations

    In commentary, passages quoted, their translated or paraphrased versions may be wrapped in q or foreign with @xml:lang if given in original language, distinct from the surrounding commentary text.
