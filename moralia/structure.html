    Chapter 3 General structure of the edition

    Each of the poems, e.g. the first morale, Citra Vinum Temulentia, is represented as two individual TEI documents, one for critical transcription and commentary, the other for transliteration. These files are stored in transcription and transliteration subcollections, respectively.

    There's a convention for naming the files and assigning their identifiers, so for transcription/I_1.xml, the corresponding file is transliteration/I_1-trlit.xml.

    Similarly, mor.I.1 is the @xml:id of the former file, and transliteration_mor.I.1 will be used for the latter.

    Every edition document will be composed of three parts: the teiHeader, standOff and text sections.

    The TEI element MUST carry an obligatory @xml:id attribute with the unique text identifier, e.g. mor.I.1.

    The div element is used as a main organizational unit in the text. Therefore, in the text's body or back sections, nothing but a series of divs may occur.

    The text and body holds the annotated transcription of the primary source.

    The standOff holds the editorial commentary on the text, where available. Transliterations may not have this section.

    <TEI xml:lang="pl" xml:id="mor.I.1">
  <teiHeader>
...
</teiHeader>
  <text>
    <body><div xml:id="structure-citra-vinum-temulentia"><head>Citra Vinum Temulentia</head>
...
</div>
...
</body>
    <standOff>
...
</standOff>
  </text>
</TEI>
