    Moralia: TEI Customization

    This TEI ODD defines a TEI customization closely tailored to the needs of the edition of Potocki's Moralia.

    The tightly constrained schema aims to represent the parallel transliteration and critical transcription of the work, enriched with extensive philological commentary.

    Designed for: Jagiellonian Digital Platform (Jagiellońska Platforma Cyfrowa)
