    Chapter 6 Facsimiles, pages, lines, and columns

    Beginnings of each page in the primary source needs to be encoded with pb.

    In this customization practice, encoding for beginnings of lines is typically skipped, with the exception of dateline and address, where layout of the source document is deemed relevant. In such cases, the lb element should be used.

    6.1 Pages

    Page beginning markers must be placed as a first node in a corresponding structural element, often div or opener, or wherever they occurr in the source text.

    Page beginnings require @facs, @n and @xml:id attributes.

    <opener>
  <pb facs="0486-008-SL-008-01r.tif" n="1"/>
</opener>

    <quote>Dwojaka jest krew: ta w żyłach i ta z żył. Pierwsza jest sokiem cielesnym,
więc badanie jej należy do fizjologów. Kto tej krwi<pb facs="3101-SL-008-attachment-03.tif" n="3"/>
przypisuje jakieś inne, poza
organicznymi, specjalne właściwości i tajemnicze moce, ten, jak widzimy, w
konsekwencji obraca miasta w zgliszcza, wyrzyna miliony ludzi i wreszcie, jak to
zobaczymy, sprowadza rzeź na własny swój szczep.</quote>

    6.2 Facsimiles

    The value of @facs should be the facsimile identifier (filename) from the Jagiellonian IIIF service.

    <div xml:id="SL_008_1" type="attachment"><pb facs="3101-SL-008-attachment-01.tif" n="1"/><head><supplied>Załącznik do listu nr 8</supplied></head>
...
</div>

    6.3 Lines

    Line beginnings should be marked just before the text occurring on the line in question. The lb element should be kept within the parent element of the text starting on that line, usually p.

    Where line beginning occurs within a word, it should be encoded in the original (not the regularized) form and additionally marked with a @break attribute with a value no.

    <choice>
  <orig>Lon<lb break="no"/>dyn</orig>
  <reg>...</reg>
</choice>

    In the Laks Correspondence, a decision was taken to only encode lines in dateline and address subsections.
