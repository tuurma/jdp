    Chapter 8 Metadata (teiHeader)

    • titleStmtto record the text title and contributors
    • sourceDesc from the header module to record information about the source document (or documents where multiple copies exist)
    • profileDesc from the header module to record the most important metadata: abstract, textClass and langUsage

    8.1 Title and contributors (titleStmt)

    In the fileDesc section of the TEI Header, the titleStmt element is used to store the title of the document, and responsibility statement for the contributors working on the text in question.

    titleStmt MUST provide the publication title of the edited text, and one or more respStmt entries listing all contributors involved in a certain role.

    <fileDesc>
  <titleStmt><title>Szymon Laks do Krystyny Bednarczyk, Paryż, 17 grudnia 1975 r.</title><title level="s">Szymon Laks Krystyna i Czesław Bednarczykowie Korespondencja</title><respStmt><resp>transkrypcja</resp><name>Jadwiga Malik</name></respStmt>
...
</titleStmt>
  <publicationStmt>
    <publisher>Jagiellońska Platforma Cyfrowa / e-editiones</publisher>
    <date>2025</date>
    <availability>
      <licence target="https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/"/>
    </availability>
  </publicationStmt>
  <sourceDesc>
...
</sourceDesc>
</fileDesc>

    8.2 Responsibility statements

    Each of the individuals involved at any stage of the preparation of the edited text has a right to be duly acknowledged with their contribution. To this end, for each role or type of contribution, irregardles if directly reflected in the source control repository, a list of name elements with all individuals involved must be provided.

    It is typical that certain individuals will appear in multiple respStmt, and equally typical that certain statements will comprise a long list of individuals.

    <respStmt>
  <resp>transkrypcja</resp>
  <name>Jadwiga Malik</name>
</respStmt>
<respStmt>
  <resp>komentarz rzeczowy</resp>
  <name>Jadwiga Malik</name>
</respStmt>
<respStmt>
  <resp>znakowanie TEI</resp>
  <name>Joanna Hałaczkiewicz</name>
  <name>Magdalena Turska</name>
</respStmt>

    8.3 Description of sources

    Each of the sources used in preparation of the edition MUST be described with msDesc (for original documents) or bibl (for earlier printed editions) in the sourceDesc section.

    <sourceDesc>
  <sourceDesc>
    <msDesc>
      <msIdentifier>
        <repository>AOPiM</repository>
        <idno type="AOPiM">sygn. 0486</idno>
      </msIdentifier>
      <msContents>
        <p>list</p>
      </msContents>
      <physDesc>
        <p>format 210×294 mm, szary papier z widocznymi znakami wodnymi, arkusz
zapisany dwustronnie na maszynie</p>
      </physDesc>
    </msDesc>
    <msDesc type="attachment" subtype="copy" status="draft" corresp="#SL_008_1">
      <msIdentifier>
        <repository>AOPiM</repository>
        <idno type="AOPiM">sygn. 3247</idno>
      </msIdentifier>
      <msContents>
        <p>kopia listu</p>
      </msContents>
      <physDesc>
        <p>format 210×297 mm, papier bibułkowy, sześć arkuszy zapisanych
jednostronnie na maszynie, poprawki czerwonym i zielonym długopisem</p>
      </physDesc>
    </msDesc>
    <bibl type="printed-edition">
      <title>Szymon Laks Krystyna i Czesław Bednarczykowie Korespondencja</title>
      <title level="s">(Materiały do Dziejów Oficyny Poetów i Malarzy, t. II)</title>
      <author>Jadwiga Malik</author>
      <publisher>Avalon</publisher>
      <placeName>Kraków</placeName>
      <date>2019</date>
      <idno type="ISBN">9788377303818</idno>
    </bibl>
  </sourceDesc>
</sourceDesc>
