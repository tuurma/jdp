    Chapter 4 Division substructure

    Each text section is represented by a div element

    Each division must have at least @type and @xml:id attributes.

    Internally, divisions may be composed of a number of structural elements:

    • opener: opening section with dateline and address
    • salute: salutation formula
    • p: ordinary paragraphs
    • quote: paragraph-level quoted passages
    • closer: closing section with closing salutation and signature
    • postscript: post scriptum

    4.1 Opening of the letter

    Opener groups together dateline, byline, and similar phrases (e.g. postal addresses) appearing as a preliminary group at the start of a letter

    4.1.1 Dateline

    Dateline contains a brief description of the place and time, etc. of letter creation.

    <dateline><placeName key="geo-2988507">Paryż</placeName>, <date when="1975-12-17">17 grudnia 1975 r.</date></dateline>

    4.1.2 Address

    Intended recipients and postal addresses are encoded with address within the opener.

    Where desired, the typographic arrangements of the source regarding the address lines may be preserved with lb.

    Formal addresses including honorifics etc. may be explicitly captured with rs.

    <address>
  <lb/>
  <rs>WPani <persName key="gnd-1257178555">Krystyna Bednarczyk</persName></rs>
  <lb/>
  <orgName key="gnd-3034063-9">Oficyna Poetów i Malarzy</orgName>
  <lb/>
  <placeName key="geo-2643743">Londyn</placeName>
</address>

    4.2 Closing of the letter

    Closer groups together salutations, signatures, and similar phrases appearing as a final group at the end of a letter.

    4.3 Salutations

    Salute contains a salutation or greeting prefixed to a division of a text, or the salutation in the closing of a letter.

    In this customization, salute is used immediately following the opener and preceding the paragraphs of the main letter body, or in the closer, where salutation formulas occur.

    In all cases, salute is considered a block level element, akin to a paragraph, therefore may not be nested in p or ab.

    <salute>Wielce Szanowna Pani,</salute>
<p>Przepraszam, że znów pozwalam sobie zaniepokoić Panią moimi małymi problemami...</p>

    <closer>
  <salute>Szczerze przyjazny</salute>
  <signed>
    <persName key="gnd-119032341">Szymon Laks</persName>
  </signed>
</closer>

    4.4 Signatures

    Signed element is used for the signatures. It typically follows a salutation formula in the closer.

    <closer>
  <salute>Proszę przyjąć wyrazy poważania i szacunku</salute>
  <signed>
    <persName key="gnd-119032341">Szymon Laks</persName>
  </signed>
</closer>
