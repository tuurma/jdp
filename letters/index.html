    UJ Correspondence: TEI customization

    This TEI ODD defines a TEI customization closely tailored to the needs of the modern correspondence collection for the UJ Correspondence blueprint.

    The tightly constrained schema aims to represent the general structure of the correspondence, enriched with annotation regarding people, places and literary works and press publications mentioned in it.

    Examples used in this customization all originate from the Laks Correspondence project. Some of the remarks reflect the specific considerations of this edition.
