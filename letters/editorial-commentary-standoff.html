    Chapter 7 Editorial commentary (standOff)

    All editorial commentary is factored out of the source transcription and stored in standOff with listAnnotation and note elements. It is linked from the transcription by means of anchor elements via @corresp attribute.

    <standOff>
  <listAnnotation><note type="end" xml:id="SL-008-note-2">A. Pragier, <title key="bibl_SL_064">Nieopatrzny krok</title>,
<title type="journal">„Wiadomości”</title> 1975, nr 44 (1544), s. 6.</note>
...
</listAnnotation>
</standOff>

..

<text>
a łamach „Wiadomości” (nr
1544 z 2 XI 75)<anchor n="1" corresp="SL-008-note-2"/></text>
